Study Resources

Simple techniques you can reuse across subjects.

25-minute focus

Choose one task, remove distractions, work for a fixed block, then take a short break.

Recall first

Close the notes and write what you remember before rereading.

Question notes

Turn headings into questions and answer them from memory.

Weekly review

Revisit older material so knowledge is not limited to the day it was learned.

Explain it simply

Try to explain a concept in plain language. Gaps become easier to notice.
